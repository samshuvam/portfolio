About Us

Engineering the Future with AI

Shuvam Singh Technologies is a research-driven AI engineering company headquartered in Nepal. Founded by Shuvam Singh — an AI systems engineer with published research in cognitive architectures, memory systems for LLMs, and aerospace AI — we bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and production-ready systems.

Our team brings deep expertise in machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and distributed systems. We partner with startups, enterprises, and research institutions to build AI solutions that are reliable, scalable, and aligned with real business objectives.
