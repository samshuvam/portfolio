# Cabin and airport observation revision

## Current request

- Removed the photographic cabin layer completely. The interior now imports the FlightGear first-class seating GLB with its original textured seats, pod partitions, tables and screens, inside the windowed cabin shell.
- The referenced Sketchfab model is not downloadable according to its public API. The alternative mesh is sourced from franck-vmd/Boeing-777-Flightgear under GPL-2.0. Original AC3D mesh, source textures, licence and conversion script are distributed in the public repository.
- Window views expand into an unobstructed panorama at the end of the scroll transition. Landscapes retain their full original 1672 × 941 resolution with lossless WebP encoding. They are no longer magnified through a narrow oval. The view fills the screen without stretching. A Fit full image / Fill screen toggle lets readers choose the complete landscape or an immersive full-screen crop. View choices crossfade through a brief soft transition; ordinary downward scrolling leaves the scene for the following section.
- SUV-1478 stays on the site's autonomous cruise route. It never becomes an airport landing aircraft. The separate airport scene has its own background arrivals and a remote departure runway.
- Replaced the aircraft chase/parking finale with a fixed observation deck. The distant camera does not zoom, chase a plane, or move into a stand. Tiny pointer parallax preserves the same overview.
- New architecture includes a sweeping roof, a broad glass concourse, rooftop gardens, five passenger piers with jet bridges, a separate arrivals hall, sculpted control tower, apron, parking and an access road. Ground vehicles and staggered aircraft animate independently.
- Three traffic callsigns are arrivals; SHUV-ACB uses the distant runway for the occasional departure. Live board states follow approach, landing and taxi. Quiet gaps and two moving aircraft slots limit congestion.
- Refined the A350 livery: pearl nose/radome, preserved original cockpit glazing, clean forebody, smaller brand lettering aft of the cockpit, evergreen tail and restrained champagne accents. Removed the stretched mural and lettering from the nose.
- Existing terminal-footer links, clock, passport and contact/CV controls remain. The build identifier is the final line of the document.

## Source and provenance

Cabin: https://github.com/franck-vmd/Boeing-777-Flightgear/tree/main/777-VMD/Models/Seating

Reference model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/airplane-interior-business-class-d6cddaa058d944c3ac41b2ed50238feb (not imported; listed as non-downloadable).

The cabin uses a licensed Boeing first-class model as a visual seating environment. It is not an exact A350 scan. Landscape images are the earlier illustrated Nepal assets, saved losslessly from their original generated PNGs. No new generated cabin photograph is used.

A350 geometry remains by amvlab under CC BY 4.0; the UV paint is generated by `scripts/build-aircraft.py`. Airport architecture is authored in Three.js. Static geometry is batched by material to reduce draw calls; aircraft and vehicles retain their own moving geometry.

Validation: module graph check, production build and ten regression tests pass. Visual checks cover the imported cabin mesh, the airport architecture from the runway side, full-screen landscapes and view controls, and a 390 × 844 phone viewport without horizontal overflow. The main plane layer fades to opacity zero in the airport deck. Production logs show no application errors.
